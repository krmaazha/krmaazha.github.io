Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) were rejecting them before a human ever read a single line.

## The Problem Nobody Talks About

Here is what the data actually looks like:

- **75% of resumes are blocked by ATS filters** before they reach a human
- A generic resume signals low intent — hiring managers spot copy-paste in seconds
- Manually tailoring a resume for one role takes **2+ hours**
- Do that for 20 roles and you have spent a full work week just rewriting your CV

The worst part? Most people do not even know they are being filtered out. They think it is a slow market, bad timing, or that they need more experience.

What they actually need is a resume that speaks the language of the ATS _and_ sounds like a human wrote it.

## What Most AI Resume Tools Get Wrong

I tested the popular AI resume tools. They all made the same mistake: they write one generic resume and call it tailored.

Here is what that looks like in practice:

- The same bullet points with a new job title swapped in
- Words like "leveraged," "spearheaded," and "synergized" — instant AI fingerprints
- No understanding of the company stage, the poster's identity, or the hiring urgency
- Outreach messages that sound like they were blasted to 500 people at once

Hiring managers are not stupid. They can spot this in seconds. And ATS systems are not looking for buzzwords — they are looking for semantic relevance.

## The 5-Agent System I Built

So I built something different. A pipeline of five specialized agents, each handling one part of the process. The goal: a resume and outreach package that reads like _you_ wrote it, because the system was built to avoid every AI cliche.

Here is how it works.

![5-Agent Pipeline](https://krmaazha.github.io/content/medium-pipeline.png)

### Agent 1: Post Analyzer

Reads the job post and extracts what actually matters — the poster's identity, company stage, role requirements, tech stack, hiring urgency, and writing tone. Not just keywords. Context.

### Agent 2: Psychology Profiler

Profiles the hiring manager's communication style and decision-making approach. Are they direct or diplomatic? Data-driven or vision-oriented? This determines the persuasion angle.

### Agent 3: Profile Matcher

Maps your skills against the role requirements, ranks your most relevant experience, extracts ATS keywords with semantic relevance (not just exact matches), and flags any gaps.

### Agent 4: Resume Writer

Generates a tailored resume in paragraph format with zero AI fingerprints. No bullet points, no hyphens, no "passionate about." It sounds like a person wrote it because it was built to reject every AI template.

### Agent 5: Message Composer

Writes a 3-to-5 sentence outreach message adapted to the platform and the poster's personality. It references a specific detail from the post so it never looks mass-sent.
