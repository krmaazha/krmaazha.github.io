## Pro Tips for LinkedIn Algorithm

- **First line is everything** — make it a pattern interrupt
- **Use line breaks** — walls of text get skipped
- **No external links in the post** — put them in the first comment instead (algorithm punishes link posts)
- **Post between 8-10 AM or 12-2 PM** in your target timezone (US Eastern for tech jobs)
- **Reply to every comment within 1 hour** — this signals engagement to the algorithm
- **Do not edit the post after publishing** — algorithm resets reach on edits
