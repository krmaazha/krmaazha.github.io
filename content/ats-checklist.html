ATS Resume Checklist 2026

The 21-point audit to get past applicant tracking systems

73% of resumes fail ATS screening before a human reads them.

This checklist covers the exact technical and content issues that cause rejections. Tick each box before submitting your next application.

Format & Structure

  • Single column only. Two-column templates scramble ATS parsers that read left-to-right linearly.
  • Standard fonts only: Arial, Calibri, Georgia, or Helvetica. Avoid custom fonts.
  • No tables, text boxes, or graphics. ATS extracts plain text. Visual elements break parsing.
  • Save as .docx (not PDF) for maximum compatibility. Some older ATS struggle with PDF parsing.
  • Use standard section headers: "Experience," "Education," "Skills." Creative headers confuse parsers.

Content & Keywords

  • Mirror the job post language. If they say "project management," do not write "PM oversight."
  • Include keywords in context, not lists. "Managed $2M budget using Agile methodology" beats "Skills: Agile, Budgeting."
  • Match 60%+ of required skills. Most ATS score relevance. Below 60% = automatic rejection.
  • Spell out acronyms first: "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" not just "SEO."
  • Use years of experience numbers. "5+ years" not "extensive experience."

Anti-AI Writing Rules

  • Banned phrases removed: "Results-driven," "spearheaded," "leveraged," "synergy," "dynamic."
  • No bullet points in the intro. Start with a 2-3 sentence summary in natural language.
  • Varied sentence length. 3-word action verbs mixed with 15-word context sentences.
  • Implied first person. "Led team of 5" not "I led a team of 5."
  • No hyphenated compound adjectives. "Fast paced environment" not "fast-paced environment."
⚠️ Hiring managers downvote AI-sounding resumes. They scan for the phrases above in 6 seconds. If detected, your resume drops to the "maybe" pile even if it passed ATS.

Contact & Personal Info

  • Professional email only. No nicknames, no numbers, no outdated domains.
  • LinkedIn URL included. 87% of recruiters check LinkedIn. Make it easy.
  • Location matches job requirements. If remote, say "Remote — [City, Country]."

Final Review

  • Read it out loud. If it sounds robotic, rewrite it.
  • Test with a plain-text viewer. Copy-paste into Notepad. If it is garbled, ATS will struggle.
  • Length: 1 page (0-5 years exp) or 2 pages (5+ years). Never exceed 2 pages for ATS.
  • Tailor for EVERY role. Generic resumes score 20-30% relevance. Tailored ones score 80%+.
