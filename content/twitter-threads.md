## Twitter/X Posting Tips

### Formatting for Engagement
- **Tweet 1 must hook in 3 seconds** — use a number, a contradiction, or a "I did X and learned Y" opener
- **Use line breaks** — never walls of text
- **One idea per tweet** — threads are for series, not paragraphs split up
- **End with clear CTA** — email address, not external links (algorithm limits reach on link tweets)

### Posting Schedule
| Day | Time (EST) | Thread |
|-----|------------|--------|
| Tuesday | 9 AM | Thread 1 (Educational) |
| Thursday | 1 PM | Thread 3 (Quick Value) |
| Saturday | 11 AM | Thread 4 (Story) |

### Engagement Hacks
1. **Reply to your own first tweet** with the thread link ("Read the full thread here ↓") after 10 minutes — this triggers notifications for people who liked tweet 1
2. **Quote tweet** job search frustration posts with a relevant tweet from your thread
3. **Reply to viral tweets** in your niche (job search, careers, tech hiring) with value first, then soft pitch
4. **Pin your best thread** to your profile

### Hashtag Strategy
- **Never use hashtags in the first tweet** — it looks automated, reduces reach
- **Use 1-2 hashtags in the final tweet only**
- Best hashtags for your niche:
  - #JobSearch
  - #ResumeTips
  - #CareerAdvice
  - #ATS
  - #TechJobs
  - #JobHunting2026

### Metrics to Track
- **Bookmark rate** — signals saved-for-later value (goal: 1%+)
- **Reply rate** — signals conversation (goal: 0.5%+)
- **Profile clicks** — signals interest in your service (track via Twitter Analytics)
